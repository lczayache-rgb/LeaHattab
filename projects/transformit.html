Project Overview

From zero to launch.

TransformIT is a tech consulting firm operating between Israel and Europe. When they came to me, they had a logo and a vision — but nothing digital.

The brief: build a premium, modern landing page that would position them as a credible player in two very different markets simultaneously.

The Challenge
01

No Existing Digital Presence

No website, no digital assets, no design system. Everything had to be built from scratch while respecting the existing visual identity.

02

Old-School Competition

After a competitive benchmark, I found most players in this space had outdated, static websites. A clear opportunity to differentiate.

03

Dual Audience: Israel & Europe

The design needed to communicate credibility and innovation to both Israeli tech professionals and European investors — two audiences with different visual expectations.

Key design decisions

Why I made these choices

Dynamic hero section

While competitors used static heroes, I designed a bold animated hero immediately communicating ambition and modernity.

ux strategy

Logo-first color system

Built the entire visual system around the existing logo palette — without changing a single element of the original identity.

brand consistency

Clear value architecture

Structured the page to answer: Who are you → What do you do → Why trust you. Reduced cognitive load and increased intent.

differentiation

Responsive-first approach

Designed mobile and desktop simultaneously to ensure coherence across every device from day one.
