The Story Behind the Brand

More than a bakery, A family memory.

Chez Suzanne is named after their grandmother — a woman who cooked with love and brought her grandchildren to "La Baule" every summer.

The brand carries her name as a tribute, a thank you, a memory kept alive.

The existing identity was a single outdated logo that no longer reflected the warmth and quality of the bakery. The goal: modernize completely while keeping the soul, the nostalgia, the family story.

The Challenge
01

No Existing Digital Presence

The client had only a logo, no website, no digital assets, no design system. Everything had to be built from scratch while respecting the existing visual identity.

02

Modernize Without Losing the Soul

The brand needed to feel contemporary and premium while preserving the warmth, the nostalgia and the family story that makes Chez Suzanne unique.

03

Consistency Across All Touchpoints

From packaging to social media to in-store signage — every element needed to feel like part of the same coherent world.
